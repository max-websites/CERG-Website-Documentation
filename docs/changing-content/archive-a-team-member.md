# Archive a team member

When a team member leaves CERG, do not delete their page.

Instead, we can archive their team member page. This removes it from the [CERG Team page :material-arrow-top-right:](https://www.sfu.ca/politics/cerg/cerg-team.html){target="_blank"} while keeping it accessible through links from other pages (e.g. working papers, events), essentially unlisting their profile rather than deleting it.

1. In AEM, navigate to the team member's page under CERG team:

    [CERG Team in AEM :material-arrow-top-right:](https://author.sfu.ca/siteadmin#/content/sfu/politics/CERG/cerg-team){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank"}

1. Right click on the team member's page and select `Page Properties`.
1. In the `Basic` tab, add the tag `unlisted` to the `Tags` field.
1. Press the `OK` button and [Activate the page](../general/update-info-on-a-page.md#while-in-wcm) to save your changes.
