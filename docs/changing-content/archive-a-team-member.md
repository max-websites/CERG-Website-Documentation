# Archive a team member

When a team member leaves CERG, simply deactivate their page. This will remove their entry from the [CERG Team page :material-arrow-top-right:](https://www.sfu.ca/politics/cerg/cerg-team.html){target="_blank"}. However, this will also remove their profile from any other pages that link to it, such as working papers or events.
